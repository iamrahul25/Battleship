Delete Account & Data

Battleship, by Rahul Kumar · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Battleship has no accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in, so there is no account to delete. We don't keep your profile or progress on a server. Everything (your call sign, avatar, stats, medals and settings) is saved only on your own device, and you can erase it yourself at any time using the steps below.

What data exists

The game keeps this data locally on your device:

  • Your profile: call sign, avatar and a random player ID
  • Your stats: wins and losses, shots, hits, ships sunk, power-ups used and win streaks
  • The medals you've unlocked
  • An unfinished game against a bot, if you have one
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, game rules, board size and so on)

During an online match, the game server holds your call sign, avatar, random player ID and online record in memory so it can run the match, and deletes them when the match ends. Short operational logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider after a limited time. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

Choose whichever option suits you. Deleted progress can't be recovered.

Option 1: Reset all data inside the game

Erases your profile, stats, medals, saved game and settings, and gives you a new random player ID.

  1. Open Battleship.
  2. On the home screen, tap Settings.
  3. Scroll down to Data and tap Reset all data.
  4. Tap Erase to confirm.

The game then starts fresh and asks you to choose a new call sign.

Option 2: Clear the app's storage (Android)

Erases everything the game has saved. The game starts fresh next time you open it.

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Battleship.
  3. Tap Storage & cache (or Storage).
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm.

Menu names vary slightly between phone makers and Android versions.

Option 3: Uninstall the game

Uninstalling Battleship removes the app and all of its saved data from your device. On Android, press and hold the app icon and tap Uninstall, or uninstall it from the Google Play Store.

Option 4: Playing in a web browser

If you play the web version, your progress is saved in your browser. Use Reset all data in the game's Settings, or clear the site data for the game's website in your browser's settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Site data).

Copies outside the game

  • Device backups: if Google backup is turned on, your device may have backed up the game's data. You can manage or delete these backups in your device or Google account settings. We don't have access to them.
  • Private room codes: if you copied a room code to share with a friend, it's only a temporary code for that room and stops working once the room closes.

Requests to the developer

Because we don't store your profile or progress, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request is needed. If you'd still like us to remove any server log entries that might include your call sign, or you want written confirmation, email us with your call sign and the approximate date you played online. We'll reply as soon as possible, and within 30 days at most.
