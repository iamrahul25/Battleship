Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version: Battleship has no account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your profile, stats, medals and settings are saved only on your own device. Games against bots never leave your phone. When you play online, the game server receives your call sign (nickname), avatar, a random player ID and your online win/loss record so it can run the match. It keeps them only in memory for that match and does not store them in a database. We never sell or share your data.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Battleship game (the "App"), developed by Rahul Kumar ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information. It applies to the App on Android and the web, to the online game server the App connects to, and to this website.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for any personal information. The App never asks for your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. There is no account to create and no sign-in of any kind.

When you first open the App you choose a call sign (a nickname of 3 to 16 characters) and an avatar from a set of built-in pictures. The App also creates a random player ID on your device. This ID is a random number; it is not linked to your device, your Google account or anything else about you.

Playing against bots happens entirely on your device and sends nothing to us. Only online matches involve our game server, as described in section 3.

2. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the App saves game data in its private storage on your device (on the web, in your browser's local storage). This data stays on your device.

Data Why it's stored
Profile: call sign, avatar, random player ID and when the profile was created To show your profile and identify you to opponents in online matches
Stats: wins and losses (online and per bot difficulty), shots, hits, ships sunk, power-ups used, win streaks and fastest wins To show your service record and work out your rank
Medals you've unlocked and when you unlocked them To show your achievements
An unfinished game against a bot So you can resume the battle later
A short-lived reconnection token for the current online match So you can rejoin a match if your connection drops
Settings: sound and music volume, haptics, turn timer, game rules, default mode, board size, obstacles and reduced motion To keep the App set up the way you like it

We cannot see, access or recover this data. If you use Reset all data, uninstall the App or clear its storage, it is permanently removed.

3. Online multiplayer

Online matches (Quick Match and Private Rooms) are run by our game server, which is hosted by Render in Singapore. The App only connects to the server when you open the Play Online screens or join a match.

What the server receives

  • Your call sign, avatar, random player ID and your online win/loss count.
  • Your game actions: where you place your ships, your shots, power-ups, quick emotes and rematch requests.
  • The game options you pick (mode, board size and obstacles) and any private room code.

What your opponent sees

Your call sign, avatar, online win/loss record, connection status and the quick emotes you send. Your ship positions stay hidden from your opponent until each ship is sunk or the match ends. There is no free-text chat; emotes are a fixed set of preset messages.

How long it is kept

The server keeps match data only in memory while the match is running and deletes it when the match room closes. It has no database and does not keep a history of your matches. The server writes short operational logs (for example, that a player with a given call sign joined a room) to help us fix problems; these logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time and then deleted automatically.

Like any internet service, the server and our hosting provider handle your IP address and basic connection details in order to deliver data to your device. We do not use them to identify or track you. Render's handling of this information is described in the Render Privacy Policy.

Tip: your call sign is visible to other players, so please don't use your real name or anything personal as your call sign.

4. Device backups

On Android, if you have turned on Google backup for your device, Android may include the App's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of your device's operating system, controlled by you in your device settings and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to these backups.

5. Device permissions

The App uses only what it needs to play:

  • Internet: to connect to the game server for online matches. Bot games work fully offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback on hits and taps. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Audio playback: to play music and sound effects. You can turn these down or off in Settings.
  • Clipboard (write only): when you tap to copy a private room code, the code is copied to your clipboard so you can share it. The App does not read your clipboard.

The App never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files, and the microphone permission is explicitly removed from the Android app.

6. Third parties, ads and analytics

  • The App shows no advertising.
  • The App contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • The only third party involved in running the App is our server host, Render, which processes online match traffic on our behalf.

The App is distributed through app stores and GitHub. Those platforms may collect information when you download the App, under their own privacy policies. This policy does not cover them.

7. Children's privacy

Battleship is a casual game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The App doesn't ask for personal details, call signs are filtered for offensive words, and there is no free-text chat with other players. If you believe a child has shared personal information through a call sign or by contacting us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Deleting your data

Since there are no accounts and nothing is stored on our side beyond the short-lived match data and logs described above, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete. You can erase all game data on your device at any time with Settings → Reset all data, by clearing the App's storage, or by uninstalling the App. Resetting also creates a new random player ID. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete Account & Data page.

9. Security

Your game data stays in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The released App connects to the game server over an encrypted connection (secure WebSocket, wss://). The server checks every message, never sends your hidden ship positions to your opponent and stores nothing permanently.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add accounts, cloud stats or leaderboards in the future), we will update this policy before that change is released and update the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
