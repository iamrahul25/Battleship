How to play

Classic rules you already know, with a few modern twists.

Deploy your fleet

Drag your ships onto the grid and tap to rotate them, or hit Random. Ships can't overlap and, by default, can't touch.

Hunt the enemy

Take turns firing into enemy waters. A hit earns you another shot, and a sunk ship is revealed for both players.

Sink them all

Hits charge your power-up meter. Spend it on special weapons and be the first to sink every enemy ship.

Features

A full naval battle in your pocket, built to look and feel great.

🤖

Three bot admirals

Cadet fires mostly at random, Captain hunts on a checkerboard and Admiral calculates where your ships most likely are. None of them cheat.

🌐

Online 1v1

Quick Match pairs you with another player, or create a Private Room and share a 6-character code with a friend. Rematch with one tap.

💥

Arsenal mode

Shaped T, L and Z ships plus five power-ups: Barrage, Sonar, Torpedo, Airstrike and Heavy Artillery. Prefer the original? Play Classic.

🗺️

Boards and obstacles

Play on boards from 7×7 to 15×15, with rocks, islands, reefs and hidden mines that make the shooter skip a turn.

🎖️

Ranks and medals

Climb from Recruit to Fleet Admiral, track accuracy and win streaks, and earn 12 medals like Sharpshooter and Untouchable.

🌊

Feels like battle

Animated water, shell flights, explosions, screen shake, sonar sweeps, sound effects, music and haptics, with a reduced-motion option.

Fair play

Same rules for everyone

Bots and online players use the exact same rules engine, and online games are refereed by the server.
